SILM Monedula

A hand-launched observation drone you print, fly and repair yourself. Long endurance, a stabilised camera and an encrypted 4G link, for about €870 in parts.

Why Monedula

01
Stays up
An efficient 1.5 m wing (lift-to-drag about 17 in CFD) and one 7000 mAh battery, aiming at about 100 minutes per flight.
02
Sees clearly
Siyi A2 mini camera on a stabilising gimbal in the nose.
03
Flies itself
ArduPilot runs the mission from launch to an automatic landing into the wind.
04
Secure link
Telemetry over 4G inside an encrypted WireGuard tunnel.
05
Fix it in the field
Every part prints on a 220 mm printer. Break one, print another overnight; the landing skids screw off.
06
No runway
Throw it by hand, land on the belly. Wings come off with two screws.

Specifications

Wingspan / length1.50 m / 0.92 m
Take-off massabout 1.8 kg
Cruise / stall speedabout 15 m/s (54 km/h) / about 10 m/s
Enduranceabout 100 min target
CameraSiyi A2 mini, gimbal-stabilised
AutopilotArduPilot on a Matek H743
Link4G LTE with WireGuard encryption
Power4S 7000 mAh, T-Motor AT2321 pusher
Launch / landingHand launch / belly on replaceable skids
Airframe3D-printed LW-PLA, PETG and TPU on a carbon spar

Monedula is designed and CFD-checked but not built yet. All figures are design estimates, not flight data.
