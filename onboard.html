Autonomy

Flying and seeing without a link

Each mission is planned on a laptop and flown by the autopilot from hand launch to landing. It recognises what it sees, saves geo-tagged photos and lands safely if GPS is lost or jammed. So far it has been tested in software and in ArduPilot's simulator, not in flight.

01 · Plan
In the Ground Station
Draw the survey area, launch point and landing strip on a map. It builds and checks the autopilot settings and the mission.
02 · Fly
No pilot needed
ArduPilot flies the route and ends every mission with an automatic landing into the wind. Low battery, GPS loss or leaving the geofence all lead to the same landing.
03 · See
On the Raspberry Pi
It looks for objects in each frame and saves a boxed photo with ground coordinates. It never steers the aircraft.
04 · Review
Every picture on a map
After landing, the flight comes back over Wi-Fi or a USB stick and shows up on a map, filtered by object type.

Buteo has no radio-control link at all. Monedula adds an ELRS receiver for control in the field, but its missions do not depend on it.

If GPS is lost or jammed

SituationWhat the aircraft does
GPS drops out or is jammedStops trusting GPS and flies on by dead reckoning, from airspeed, heading and the wind it measured earlier.
Still bad after 5 secondsEnds the survey and flies the landing approach.
A false or weak signal returnsTrusts GPS again only after 10 good seconds that agree with its own estimate.
The estimate drifts too farA geofence 100 m beyond the route sends it to the landing.
Lands with GPS still goneSwitches the motor off for good after 15 seconds standing still.

In the simulator, landings without GPS overshot by 85 m in 8 m/s of wind and 107 m in 10 m/s, so the Ground Station will not launch above 10 m/s.

Recognition

Our ready-made model knows tractors, cars, trucks and livestock. On drone pictures it had never seen, it found 8 to 9 in 10 of the cattle and sheep. Size is the hard limit: the Ground Station picks the highest altitude at which the smallest target is still big enough, about 80 m for cars. Vehicles seen from straight above are still weak; we will improve them with our own flight pictures.

400 automated tests, more than 40 GPS-loss scenarios and 6 of 6 jammed missions in ArduPilot's simulator pass. Only flight tests will show whether that holds in the air.
