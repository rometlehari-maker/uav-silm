      How a flight works

      1. Aircraft - answer the questions about your aircraft once.
      2. Mission - draw the area on the map, set where you launch and land, enter today's wind.
      3. Camera - choose what it should look for.
      4. Send to aircraft - USB cable to the flight controller; Wi-Fi or a USB stick to the aircraft's computer.
      5. Launch - tick the checklist, arm, throw. It flies, lands and switches itself off.
      6. Flights & pictures - bring the pictures back and see where each one was taken.

      Before the first real flight, fly your mission in the Simulator (step 7).

      Your aircraft

      Send to the aircraft

      Two parts of the aircraft get your answers: the flight controller (flies the aircraft; USB cable) and the aircraft computer (the Raspberry Pi that takes the pictures; its own Wi-Fi, or a USB stick). Do both after every change. The aircraft must be disarmed.

      A · Flight controller (USB cable)

      Which USB port is the flight controller plugged into?

      Plug the USB cable into the flight controller and the battery in. Windows shows it as COM3, COM4…; Linux as /dev/ttyACM0.

      After steps 1-2 the first time, unplug the battery and USB for 10 s (reboot) so the flight controller starts the script.

      Or use Mission Planner / QGroundControl instead

      Download the files and load them there (parameters: CONFIG > Full Parameter List > Load from file; mission: PLAN > Load).

      B · Aircraft computer over Wi-Fi

      Power the aircraft, wait one minute, then join this laptop to the aircraft's Wi-Fi network (named SILM-…, with the password chosen when the Pi was set up).
